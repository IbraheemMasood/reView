    Decompiler cheat sheetOpen
    • param_1, param_2… function arguments, in order.
    • local_XX, iVar1, pcVar2… local variables. Prefixes hint at types.
    • undefined8 an 8-byte value Ghidra could not identify.
    • FUN_00101234 an unnamed function. Rename it after you understand it.
    • in_FS_OFFSET + 0x28 a stack canary check, often safe to ignore.
    • 0x21, 0x1505… hexadecimal constants. Convert them or look them up.
    • Quoted strings often reveal useful clues.
    • Decompiler output is a reconstruction. The original source looked different.
